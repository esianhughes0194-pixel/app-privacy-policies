Turn Tracker

Privacy Policy

Effective 9 September 2026 Applies to iOS app Contact esianhughes0194@gmail.com

The short version: Turn Tracker doesn't collect your data. Everything a family enters — kids' names and turn lists — stays on that one device. There's no account, no server, and nothing to leak.

01

What we collect

Turn Tracker is built to work entirely on your device. The table below covers everything the app stores, and where it goes.

DataWhere it's stored
Kids' namesOn this device only
Turn lists & rotation orderOn this device only
Turn historyOn this device only
Face ID / Touch IDNever leaves iOS
Name, email, contacts, locationNot collected
Analytics or advertising dataNot collected
02

Face ID & Touch ID

If you turn it on in Settings, Turn Tracker can ask iOS to check Face ID or Touch ID before a parent advances the turn. That check happens entirely inside iOS's own Secure Enclave — the app only ever receives a yes or no. We never see, store, or have access to any biometric data.

03

No ads, no analytics, no tracking

  • Turn Tracker has no advertising SDKs and shows no ads.
  • It doesn't use analytics or crash-reporting tools that phone home.
  • It doesn't track you across other apps or websites.
  • It has no login, so there's no account to be tied to your identity.
04

Children's privacy (COPPA & GDPR)

Turn Tracker is a turn-taking tool that a parent or guardian sets up and manages for their family, including any children who use it. We built it with children's privacy as a first principle, in line with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU's General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the App Store's Kids guidelines:

  • No personal information is collected, transmitted, or shared — from children or adults. Kids' names, turn lists, and rotation history are typed in by the parent or generated by using the app, and saved only in the app's local storage on that device. We have no servers, no backend, and no database, so we never receive, see, or store this information ourselves.
  • We don't ask for parental contact information — no email, phone number, or address is collected from a parent or a child, for consent purposes or otherwise.
  • No third-party analytics or advertising. Turn Tracker contains no third-party analytics SDKs, ad networks, or tracking libraries of any kind — see Section 03. Nothing about how a child uses the app is ever shared with a third party.
  • A parent or guardian is always in control. The adult who installs the app sets up each turn list, can view or edit any child's data, and can permanently delete a child's information at any time from Settings → Remove Kid.

Because no data ever leaves the device, there is nothing for us to disclose to, or delete on request from, any third party — removing a kid, a turn list, or the app itself is immediate and complete. If you have questions about how Turn Tracker handles a child's information, contact us using the details in Section 07.

05

Deleting your data

You're always in control. Remove a kid from Settings → Remove Kid, or delete a whole turn list, to erase their rotation and history for good. Deleting the app removes everything else immediately, since nothing is kept anywhere but your device.

06

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for instance, if a future update adds an optional online feature — we'll update the effective date above and describe the change here before it applies.
